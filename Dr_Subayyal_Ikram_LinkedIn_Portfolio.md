## About — a purposeful introduction

Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Raheem.

I am Dr. Subayyal Ikram, Chief Executive Officer of ABS Developers in Lahore. Real estate is my professional focus; faith shapes how I think about responsibility, relationships and the purpose behind my work.

My first professional chapter was medicine. In respect of my mother's wish, I studied at Dow University of Health Sciences and completed my MBBS in 2014. I went on to work as a House Officer at Services Institute of Medical Sciences and a House Surgeon at Jinnah Hospital, Lahore. Entrepreneurship, however, had long held my interest, and I eventually followed that calling into property development.

At ABS, I want the ambition to create better living spaces to be matched by care in our dealings. The company's published approach includes equal cash and instalment prices, no late-payment penalties and a full-refund policy. For me, the trust placed in a business carries a responsibility to communicate honestly and take quality seriously.

There have been difficult chapters, too. A major financial setback challenged my family and my confidence. I found reassurance in the Quran, particularly the closing reminder in Surah At-Talaq, 65:4. That period deepened the importance of patience, trust in Allah and the willingness to start again.

Alongside my work, I share Quran recitations and speak about the relationship between faith and daily choices. Online prayer recordings introduced my recitation to a wider audience; interviews, religious television and public talks became further ways to share that message.

I encourage young people to be ambitious about what they can contribute, careful about how they earn and truthful in what they promise. My aspiration is to keep learning, serve responsibly and seek Allah's pleasure through the decisions that make up an ordinary working day.

I welcome conversations about real estate, entrepreneurship, youth development and bringing Islamic values into professional life.
